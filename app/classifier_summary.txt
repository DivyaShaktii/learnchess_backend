==================================================
CHESS ENGINE MOVE CLASSIFICATION LOGIC
==================================================

1. THE OPENING PHASE (Ply 1-3)
--------------------------------------------------
During the very start of the game, the classifier uses special logic instead of heavy engine analysis:

* Edge Pawn Warning (OPENING_PAWN_WARNING):
  If a player moves an 'a', 'b', 'g', or 'h' pawn, it gets intercepted immediately. 
  It triggers the red "Are you sure?" box, and the coach will say: "Build your minor pieces."

* Standard Book Moves (Opening Principle): 
  If a player plays a recognized opening move defined in BOOK_MOVES_UCI (like moving an 'e' or 'd' pawn, or a knight), the coach labels it an "Opening Principle".
  It will randomly say either "Build your minor pieces." or "Develop your center" from the OPENING_MESSAGES list.

2. HIGH-LEVEL TACTICAL SACRIFICES
--------------------------------------------------
For the rest of the game, before checking standard engine thresholds, the classifier checks for "genius" moves using _detect_high_level_tactic:

* The Condition: It only triggers if a player purposefully sacrifices a Queen or a Rook on a square where it can be captured by the opponent.
* Brilliant: Awarded if the sacrifice leads to a massive advantage (like +6.00 points) or a forced checkmate.
* Best Move: Awarded if the sacrifice leads to a strong advantage (+3.00 points).
* Excellent: Awarded if the sacrifice is mathematically sound but doesn't lead to a huge swing.

3. STANDARD ENGINE EVALUATION (Centipawn Loss)
--------------------------------------------------
If a move isn't in the opening book and isn't a crazy Queen/Rook sacrifice, it falls back to standard Stockfish analysis. The engine compares the move the player made to the absolute best move possible. 

The difference in score is called "Centipawn Loss" (100 loss = 1 pawn blunder). It categorizes them based on these thresholds:

* Good: Loss is under 25 (Player found a perfectly fine move).
* Inaccuracy: Loss is under 60 (Player missed something better. Triggers Red Box).
* Mistake: Loss is under 150 (Player blundered a pawn or positional advantage. Triggers Red Box).
* Blunder: Loss is under 300 (Player blundered a minor piece like a Knight/Bishop. Triggers Red Box).
* Worst Move: Loss is over 300 (Player hung their Queen or got checkmated. Triggers Red Box).
